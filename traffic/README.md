#### My Experimentation Process
In the get_model function I used the same code as was given in week 5 in handwriting.py, even the model.compile was the same I used adam and categorical_crossentropy then I started to experiment.
I tried changing number of filters, kernal and the amount of nodes in the hidden layer. I started with hidden layer thinking of since in handwriting.py when I used grayscale I had 128 nodes in the hidden layer, I thought since in this project I am using RGB which is 3 colors I should try to increase the nodes from 128 to 384. The performance increased but I got scared of overfitting so I left it for now (used it later when I understood how to know if the results are good from overfitting or not), I tried changing the number of filters and kernal to 3 times of what it was, form 32, (3, 3) to 96, (9, 9), That obviously didn't work so I went on to Dropout increasing it while I added another layer and the accuracy was still not good. 
I then finally started to look at model.compile where I first kept adam but changed the learning rate, accuracy got worse so I tried to change optimizer, I tried every single optimizer in the keras site (except for Lamb, Loss Scale Optimizer and Muon I didn't bother with them, since I couldn't run it in the terminal) and found out the best 2 gave better results is RMSprop and Ftrl. I sticked to RMSprop because it gave better accuracy. 
I went on to losses, at first I tried everything in Hinge losses for "maximum-margin" classification and some in Probabilistic (in probabilistic the first the I looked at was everything that had Categorical in the name), I didn't try regression and later found out Hinge is not meant for image classification tasks. Anyways after trying most of the losses out to see which one decreases losses and increases accuracy and found out the best 3 (better then categorical_crossentropy and out of all of them) were: 1- BinaryCrossentropy. 2- Poisson. 3- categorical_focal_crossentropy. BinaryCrossentropy being the best amongst them given amazing accuracy and very low losses (I tested most of them 2 times atleast and the 3 best I tested 4 times for more accurate results).
I sticked to RMSprop as my optimizer and BinaryCrossentropy for the loss. then finally got back to the hidden layers and increased it to 384 nodes which gave amazing results given accuracy: 0.9728 - loss: 0.0112
